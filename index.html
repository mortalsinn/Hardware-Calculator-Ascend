What actually breaks first when you scale

Drag the seats. Most capacity planners show you CPU and RAM — for AscendOS those stay almost flat, because the backend spends its life waiting on external AI calls, not computing. Three things do move: the inference bill, Qdrant's memory, and how many long-lived scan connections one process can hold. This models all of it, and says which numbers are measured and which are assumptions you should replace.
