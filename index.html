What it costs to run AscendOS, from 1 seat to a million

Costs are modelled from how AscendOS actually works — every AI call each module makes, how long a scan holds a connection, how much index each region needs — priced against three real hosting architectures. Servers barely move as you grow; the backend spends its life waiting on AI calls rather than computing. What grows is the inference bill, and that is where every decision on this page should be aimed.
